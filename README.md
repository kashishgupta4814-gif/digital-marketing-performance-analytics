# Digital Marketing Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing digital marketing performance using Python and Google Colab. The objective is to understand how marketing spending influences revenue, customer conversions, campaign performance and overall marketing efficiency.

The analysis uses a marketing spending dataset containing campaign-level information such as campaign name, category, impressions, leads, revenue and marketing spend.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall marketing spending and revenue.
- Evaluate customer lead and conversion performance.
- Compare different marketing campaigns.
- Calculate Return on Marketing Investment (ROMI).
- Analyze marketing cost efficiency.
- Study the relationship between marketing spending and revenue.
- Perform regression analysis to understand the impact of marketing spending on revenue.
- Identify campaigns with better marketing performance.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Marketing Spending Analysis
Analyzed total, average, maximum and minimum marketing spending and compared spending across campaigns.

### 2. Revenue Analysis
Analyzed total revenue, average revenue, maximum and minimum revenue and evaluated revenue performance across campaigns.

### 3. Customer Conversion Analysis
Analyzed leads and conversions to understand how effectively marketing activities generated customers.

### 4. Campaign Performance
Compared campaigns using key marketing metrics such as spending, revenue, leads, conversions and efficiency.

### 5. ROMI Analysis
Calculated Return on Marketing Investment to identify campaigns generating stronger returns from marketing expenditure.

### 6. Cost Efficiency Analysis
Compared marketing spending with leads, conversions and revenue to evaluate cost efficiency.

### 7. Marketing Spend vs Revenue
Examined the relationship between marketing expenditure and revenue using data visualization.

### 8. Regression Analysis
Applied regression analysis to study whether marketing spending can help explain or predict revenue.

### 9. Geographic Analysis
The dataset did not contain a geography/location column, so geographic analysis was not included in the final analysis.

### 10. Marketing Performance Dashboard
Created a dashboard-style view of important marketing performance indicators to support business decision-making.

## Key Business Questions

- Which campaigns generate the highest revenue?
- Which campaigns generate the most leads?
- Which campaigns have better conversion performance?
- Which campaigns provide better ROMI?
- Which campaigns are more cost-efficient?
- Is higher marketing spending associated with higher revenue?
- How can marketing resources be allocated more effectively?
